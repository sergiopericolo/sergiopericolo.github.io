Life Insurance Premiums and Reserves

Lectures 14–16 of Financial Mathematics (University of Brescia). Discounting meets survival. A premium is fair when its value equals the expected present value of what the insurer pays. Commutation symbols turn every contract into a ratio of table entries.

Roadmap:

  1. Equivalence principle — net premium, single and periodic.
  2. Pure endowment — \({}_m E_x\), and the symbol \(D_x\).
  3. Life annuities — \(a_x\), \(\ddot a_x\), deferred and temporary; the symbol \(N_x\).
  4. Death benefits — one-year, whole life, deferred, term; the symbols \(C_x\), \(M_x\).
  5. Endowment insurance — survival + death cover.
  6. Periodic premiums and the mathematical reserve.

Equivalence principle and net premium

A contract is fair when the expected present value of the premiums paid by the insured equals the expected present value of the benefits paid by the insurer.

  • Net premium (premio puro): the premium from this principle, with no loadings for costs or profit.
  • Net single premium (premio unico puro): paid once, at signing.
  • Periodic premium: paid in several instalments.
  • Gross premium (premio di tariffa): net premium + loadings.

Pure endowment

A person aged \(x\) pays a single premium \(P\) now. The insurer pays \(C\) at age \(x + m\) if the person is alive; nothing otherwise.

Let \(X\) be the present value of the benefit:

Scenario \(X\) Probability
alive at \(x+m\) \(C v^m\) \({}_m p_x\)
dead before \(x+m\) \(0\) \({}_{/m} q_x\)

By equivalence, \(P = E(X)\):

\[P = C v^m {}_m p_x\]

For \(C = 1\) this is the pure endowment factor:

\[{}_m E_x = v^m {}_m p_x = (1+i)^{-m} {}_m p_x\]

It is a financial discount (\(v^m\)) times a demographic discount (\({}_m p_x\)). So \({}_m E_x \le v^m\): survival risk makes the payment cheaper.

Commutation symbols

Define

\[D_x = v^x l_x\]

Then

\[{}_m E_x = v^m \frac{l_{x+m}}{l_x} = \frac{v^{x+m} l_{x+m}}{v^x l_x} = \frac{D_{x+m}}{D_x}, \qquad P = C \frac{D_{x+m}}{D_x}\]

The other symbols:

Symbol Definition Used for
\(D_x\) \(v^x l_x\) survival benefits
\(N_x\) \(D_x + D_{x+1} + \dots + D_{\omega-1}\) life annuities
\(C_x\) \(v^{x+1} d_x\) death benefits
\(M_x\) \(C_x + C_{x+1} + \dots + C_{\omega-1}\) death benefits over many years

Life annuity (immediate, in arrears)

The insurer pays 1 at the end of each year while the person is alive. If death happens in year \(h+1\) (between \(x+h\) and \(x+h+1\)) the person has received \(h\) payments, worth \(\sum_{k=1}^{h} v^k\), with probability \({}_{h/1} q_x\). The net single premium is the expected value:

\[a_x = \sum_{h=1}^{\omega-x-1} \left( \sum_{k=1}^{h} v^k \right) {}_{h/1} q_x\]

Regrouping by powers of \(v\):

\[a_x = v p_x + v^2 {}_2 p_x + \dots = \sum_{h=1}^{\omega-x-1} v^h {}_h p_x = \sum_{h=1}^{\omega-x-1} {}_h E_x\]

A life annuity is a sum of pure endowments, one for each future year. With commutation symbols:

\[a_x = \frac{D_{x+1} + D_{x+2} + \dots + D_{\omega-1}}{D_x} = \frac{N_{x+1}}{D_x}\]

Other life annuities

Annuity (unit payment) Symbol Net single premium
immediate, in advance \(\ddot a_x = 1 + a_x\) \(\frac{N_x}{D_x}\)
deferred \(m\) years, in arrears \({}_{m/} a_x = {}_m E_x a_{x+m}\) \(\frac{N_{x+m+1}}{D_x}\)
deferred \(m\) years, in advance \({}_{m/} \ddot a_x\) \(\frac{N_{x+m}}{D_x}\)
temporary \(m\) years, in arrears \({}_{/m} a_x = a_x - {}_{m/} a_x\) \(\frac{N_{x+1} - N_{x+m+1}}{D_x}\)
temporary \(m\) years, in advance \({}_{/m} \ddot a_x\) \(\frac{N_x - N_{x+m}}{D_x}\)

Death benefits

One-year death cover

Pays 1 at the end of the year of death, only if death happens between \(x+h\) and \(x+h+1\). Payment at \(h+1\), probability \({}_{h/1} q_x = {}_h p_x q_{x+h}\):

\[{}_{h/1} A_x = v^{h+1} \frac{d_{x+h}}{l_x} = \frac{v^{x+h+1} d_{x+h}}{v^x l_x} = \frac{C_{x+h}}{D_x}\]

Whole life

Pays 1 at the end of the year of death, whenever it happens. It is the sum of all one-year covers:

\[A_x = \frac{C_x + C_{x+1} + \dots + C_{\omega-1}}{D_x} = \frac{M_x}{D_x}\]

Deferred and term covers

Death cover (unit capital) Pays if death occurs Net single premium
deferred \(m\) years, whole life, \({}_{m/}A_x\) after \(m\) years \(\frac{M_{x+m}}{D_x}\)
term \(m\) years, \({}_{/m}A_x\) within \(m\) years \(\frac{M_x - M_{x+m}}{D_x}\)
deferred \(m\), term \(m\), \({}_{m/m}A_x\) between \(m\) and \(2m\) years \(\frac{M_{x+m} - M_{x+2m}}{D_x}\)

Endowment insurance

Endowment (mista semplice): pays 1 at \(x + m\) if alive, or 1 at the end of the year of death if death comes first. Pure endowment + term cover:

\[A_{x:\overline{m}|} = \frac{D_{x+m}}{D_x} + \frac{M_x - M_{x+m}}{D_x} = \frac{D_{x+m} + M_x - M_{x+m}}{D_x}\]

Double endowment (mista a capitale raddoppiato): whole-life death cover plus an extra 1 at \(x + m\) if alive:

\[P = \frac{D_{x+m} + M_x}{D_x}\]

Periodic premiums

Often the insured pays periodic premiums instead of a single one. By equivalence, the expected PV of the premiums equals the expected PV of the benefits. Constant premiums \(\Pi\) paid in advance for at most \(n\) years, while alive, are a temporary life annuity in advance:

\[\Pi \cdot {}_{/n}\ddot a_x = \text{net single premium of the benefits}\]

Mathematical reserve

The mathematical reserve is the insurer's debt to the insured at a time \(k > 0\) after signing. With level premiums, the early premiums exceed the risk covered (death is unlikely when young). The surplus is set aside to pay for later years, when death becomes more likely.

Two equivalent ways to compute it:

  1. Prospective (look forward): expected PV at \(k\) of the future benefits minus expected PV of the future premiums.

\[V_{x+k} = \text{PV}_{x+k}(\text{insurer}) - \text{PV}_{x+k}(\text{insured})\]

  1. Retrospective (look back): expected PV of the premiums already paid minus the benefits already paid, accumulated to \(k\) with the actuarial factor \(1/{}_k E_x\).

\[V_{x+k} = \left[ \text{PV}_x(\text{insured}) - \text{PV}_x(\text{insurer}) \right] \cdot \frac{1}{{}_k E_x}\]

Key takeaways

  • Equivalence: expected PV of premiums = expected PV of benefits → net premium.
  • \({}_m E_x = v^m {}_m p_x = D_{x+m}/D_x\), always \(\le v^m\).
  • \(a_x = \sum_h {}_h E_x = N_{x+1}/D_x\); \(\ddot a_x = N_x/D_x\); deferred and temporary by differences of \(N\).
  • \({}_{h/1}A_x = C_{x+h}/D_x\); \(A_x = M_x/D_x\); term and deferred by differences of \(M\).
  • Endowment \(= (D_{x+m} + M_x - M_{x+m})/D_x\).
  • Reserve: prospective (future benefits − future premiums) = retrospective (past premiums − past benefits, accumulated by \(1/{}_k E_x\)).
